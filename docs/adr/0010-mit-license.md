# 0010. Licence: MIT

- **Status:** Accepted (confirmed by Mark on 2026-11-19)
- **Date:** 2026-11-19

## Context

A design system is copied into many products and, with `eject`, into their source trees, so its
licence must be simple and permissive.

## Decision

Unison is released under the **MIT** licence (`LICENSE` at the repo root; `"license": "MIT"` in
every public package).

## Alternatives considered

- **Apache-2.0:** adds an explicit patent grant, but is longer and less common in this space.
- **A copyleft licence:** would block most commercial adopters.

## Consequences

Anyone can use, modify and redistribute Unison, including ejected component source, as long as the
copyright notice is kept.
